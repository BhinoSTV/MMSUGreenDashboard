# SDG 15 (Life on Land) — THE Sustainability Impact Ratings evidence pack

Prepared 2026-10-06 for the 2027 data cycle (submission due **9 Nov 2026**, per THE).
Rules source: THE Sustainability Impact Ratings Methodology 2026 v1.0, SDG 15
(indicators 15.2.1–15.4.3). The 2027 methodology must be checked for the data year.

> **Nothing in this folder is evidence yet.** Each file is a draft built only from
> public sources found online (listed per file). Facts marked `[CONFIRM: …]` could not
> be verified. Before uploading, the responsible MMSU unit must confirm every fact,
> add the missing dates and numbers, and issue the final version on university
> letterhead. Draft policy text is a proposal for adoption, not an existing policy.
> Submitting a policy that has not been adopted would misstate MMSU's position, and
> THE cross-checks submissions.

## What THE will accept (recap)

- One **self-contained** document or public page per indicator. Reviewers (an LLM,
  then humans) do **not** follow links, and pages with many links are rejected.
  A document whose substance is "see this URL" will score poorly.
- Per indicator: existence (1 pt), evidence relevance (specific 1 / general 0.5 /
  irrelevant 0), public (1 pt). Policy indicators add 1 pt if created or **reviewed
  in 2020–2024**, so the document must show a date.
- Data year for the 2026 edition: the calendar year 2024, or the academic or
  financial year ending in 2024. Pick one definition and use it for every indicator.
- Uploads: .pdf, .doc, Excel, .gif, .jpeg, .png (no .zip/.rar/.txt, no video or audio).
  Public online evidence must stay live until the next collection cycle.

## Findings that change the plan

1. **MMSU already publishes THE evidence PDFs** under `mmsu.edu.ph/resources/<id>/data`,
   named by indicator (e.g. "14.5.3 MMSU's Coastal Cleanup",
   "Sustainable Management of Land for Tourism"). Check what is already filed for
   SDG 15 before creating new files.
2. **The existing 15.2.6 file is link-only.** It has two paragraphs on the health
   tourism project plus one news URL, with no dates, numbers or audience. Under the
   2026 rules the reviewer does not open the URL, and the project is not community
   education on land management for tourism. Rewrite it (see `15.2.6`).
3. **Most found activities fall outside a 2024 window.** Mini-forest (Aug 2020),
   IEG mangrove work (Oct–Nov 2021), Earth Day (Apr 2022) and BCLC planting
   (Jan 2023) predate it. The Aug 2023 planting falls in AY 2023–24 only if MMSU
   uses the academic-year definition. Collect 2024 (and 2025, if the 2027 edition
   asks for it) records for each unit.
4. **The policy questions are the largest gap.** The six four-point policy
   questions (15.2.2, 15.3.1, 15.3.2, 15.3.4, 15.4.2, 15.4.3) are worth 24 of the 48
   points, and 15.4.1 adds 3 more. No public policy was found for 15.2.2, 15.3.1,
   15.3.2, 15.3.4 or 15.4.1. Either MMSU has these in Board of Regents or
   office-order records, in which case retrieve them, or it needs to adopt them.
   The drafts in this folder are for adoption and only count once approved.
5. **Not read:** the MMSU Environmental Policies page, the SDG page and the
   Sustainability page returned empty or errors in this session, as did the revised
   IPWMP. They may already contain the missing policies. Retrieve them first.
