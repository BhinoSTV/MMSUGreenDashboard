# 15.3.2 Policy: identify, monitor and protect IUCN Red List and national-list species

Max 4 pts (exists 1, evidence 1, public 1, created/reviewed 2020–2024 1) ·
In place by 2024 · **Status: Gap (no public policy found)**

## What was found

MMSU researchers have used IUCN criteria before. A 2003 survey in Laoag City, Batac,
Pasuquin, San Nicolas and Dingras recorded 124 plants perceived as vanishing; 77 were
validated by MMSU agronomists and horticulturists using IUCN criteria (45 endangered,
32 vulnerable). This shows capability, but it is 20+ years old and is research, not a
policy. It cannot satisfy this question. The question is about species with habitats in
areas affected by the university's operations.

## Draft policy for adoption (not valid until approved and dated)

**Mariano Marcos State University — Policy on Threatened Species Identification,
Monitoring and Protection**
Approved: `[CONFIRM]` · Resolution No.: `[CONFIRM]` · Review due: `[CONFIRM: ≤3 years]`

1. **Purpose.** Identify, monitor and protect species on the IUCN Red List and on
   Philippine national lists of threatened species whose habitats are affected by
   university operations.
2. **Scope.** University campuses, farms, woodlots, coastal and mangrove sites
   managed with partners. `[CONFIRM: sites]`
3. **Statements.**
   a. Maintain a baseline inventory of flora and fauna on university land, screened
      against the IUCN Red List and the DENR national lists (plants DAO 2017-11;
      fauna DAO 2019-09 — `[CONFIRM: current versions with counsel]`).
   b. Record sightings and plantings of listed species in a register updated each year.
   c. Before construction or land-use change, check the register and avoid or mitigate
      impacts on listed species; report any listed-species find to DENR.
   d. Conserve listed species ex situ where appropriate (Clonal Nursery,
      gene bank, indigenous plant collections).
4. **Responsibilities.** CAFSD (inventory lead), IEG, Planning and Strategic Foresight,
   General Services, `[CONFIRM: others]`.
5. **Monitoring and review.** Annual species register published in the sustainability
   report; policy reviewed every three years.
6. **Approval block:** `[CONFIRM]`.
