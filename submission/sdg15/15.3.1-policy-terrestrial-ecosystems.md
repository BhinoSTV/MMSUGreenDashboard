## Draft policy for adoption (not valid until approved and dated)

**Mariano Marcos State University — Policy on the Conservation, Restoration and
Sustainable Use of Terrestrial Ecosystems**
Approved: `[CONFIRM]` · Resolution No.: `[CONFIRM]` · Review due: `[CONFIRM: ≤3 years]`

1. **Purpose.** Conserve, restore and sustainably use the terrestrial ecosystems on
   university land and those the university manages with partners, including forests,
   upland and dryland areas.
2. **Scope.** All six campuses, the Biodiversity Conservation Learning Center, Salinged
   Park, university farms and woodlots, and partner sites (nipa and mangrove sites
   under the National Greening Program). `[CONFIRM: site list and hectares]`
3. **Statements.**
   a. Maintain a register of university land and its ecosystem type and condition.
   b. No clearing of native tree cover without an approved replacement plan
      (`[CONFIRM: ratio]`, using native species).
   c. Prioritise native species in planting; track survival and maintenance.
   d. Restore degraded university land through planting, soil conservation and water
      management.
   e. Align with national law and DENR rules (`[CONFIRM: citations with counsel]`).
4. **Responsibilities.** President (approval), CAFSD / IEG (technical),
   Planning and Strategic Foresight (campus plan), General Services (maintenance).
5. **Monitoring and review.** Annual land and tree-cover report; review every
   three years.
6. **Approval block:** `[CONFIRM]`.
