# 15.2.2 Policy: food on campus is sustainably farmed

Max 4 pts (exists 1, evidence 1, public 1, created/reviewed 2020–2024 1) ·
In place by 2024 · **Status: Gap (no public policy found)**

## What was found

Nothing that is a policy. Related activity exists (CAFSD Clonal Nursery, the Aug 2020
mini-forest and resiliency garden with the Department of Agriculture, the MMSU CARES
demonstration farm), but THE asks for a **policy** on food served or sold on campus.
First check whether canteen, concessionaire or university-farm-procurement rules
already exist.

## Draft policy for adoption (not valid until approved and dated)

**Mariano Marcos State University — Sustainable Food Sourcing Policy**
Approved: `[CONFIRM: date]` · Resolution/Order No.: `[CONFIRM]` · Review due: `[CONFIRM: ≤3 years]`

1. **Purpose.** Ensure food served, sold or procured on MMSU campuses is produced
   using sustainable farming practices that protect soil, water and biodiversity.
2. **Scope.** University canteens and concessionaires, events and catering funded by
   the university, university farm produce, and university food procurement.
3. **Policy statements.**
   a. Prefer produce from the university's own farms (CAFSD, CARES) and local
      farmers practising organic, natural or good-agricultural-practice systems
      (`[CONFIRM: certification basis, e.g. Philippine GAP, organic certification]`).
   b. `[CONFIRM]` Concession contracts include a sourcing clause and a reporting duty.
   c. University farms avoid prohibited pesticides and promote soil-health
      practices (composting, bio-inoculants).
   d. Food-waste reduction and composting follow the Integrated Pollution and Waste
      Management Program.
4. **Responsibilities.** Office of the President (approval); CAFSD (technical
   standards); General Services (concession compliance); Planning and Strategic
   Foresight (annual reporting).
5. **Monitoring and review.** Annual compliance report; policy reviewed at least
   every three years.
6. **Approval block.** President, Board of Regents Secretary: `[CONFIRM]`.

## Confirm before submitting

Adopt the policy, put the approval date on its face, and file as one PDF. If an
equivalent policy exists in the Board of Regents or office-order records, submit that
instead and discard this draft.
