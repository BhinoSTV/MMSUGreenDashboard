# 15.4.2 Policy on reducing plastic waste on campus

Max 4 pts (exists 1, evidence 1, public 1, created/reviewed 2020–2024 1) ·
In place by 2024 · **Status: Partial**

## Evidence statement (draft)

MMSU manages waste, including plastic, under its Integrated Pollution and Waste
Management Program (IPWMP) and unit-level measures.

- **University program.** The University Waste Management Committee was created by a
  Special Order of the President and the program was approved by the Board of Regents
  in 2009, consistent with RA 9003 and PD 1152. The IPWMP is led by the Pollution
  Control Unit (PCU) and General Services. `[CONFIRM: date of the revised IPWMP and its
  plastic clauses]`
- **Unit measure, 15 Oct 2024.** The College of Agriculture, Food and Sustainable
  Development (CAFSD) launched its Waste and Pollution Management Program: waste
  reduction, reuse and recycling; a Materials Recovery Facility; a composting facility;
  and a **ban on plastic bottled water in the college's auxiliary buildings**, with
  reusable tumblers promoted.
- **University-wide policy.** `[CONFIRM: office order or resolution extending the
  plastic measures to all campuses, with date]`
