# 15.3.3 Local biodiversity included in planning and development

Max 3 pts · Year: 2024 · **Status: Partial**

## Evidence statement (draft)

MMSU includes local biodiversity in how it plans and develops campus land.

- **Native-species design.** The Biodiversity Conservation Learning Center and Leisure
  Park on the Batac main campus is planted with native species (120 seedlings of 28

  a health and wellness tourism site while serving as a field laboratory.
