# 15.3.5 Collaboration with the local community to maintain shared land ecosystems

Max 3 pts · Year: 2024 · **Status: Strong, needs a 2024-window record**

## Evidence statement (draft)

MMSU collaborates with local governments, NGOs and communities to maintain ecosystems
that the university shares with its neighbours.

| Period | Collaboration | Partners | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct–Dec 2021 | Area Specific Action Plan (ASAP) green projects, through the MMSU Institute of Environmental Governance (IEG), CAFSD | Tanggol Kalikasan Inc.; local governments of Badoc, Pinili, Currimao, Paoay, Laoag City, Bacarra, Pasuquin, Burgos, Bangui, Pagudpud | 10,000+ mangrove seedlings on 28 ha at Pasuquin (7 Oct 2021); 200 on 2 ha at Currimao; ~380 bamboo propagules at Bangui; ~100 seedlings for riverbank stabilisation at Pinili |
| Recent (reported Dec 2025) | Coastal forest conservation project, Barangay Davila, Pasuquin, under a tripartite agreement | Municipal government of Pasuquin, MMSU, TMP Foundation (Toyota Motor Philippines Foundation); DENR-CENRO Bangui; MENRO; local community | Two-year project, target 3,500 native mangrove seedlings (lapis-lapis, arinaya) on 2 ha; 1,750 planted with about 110 volunteers including MMSU College of Industrial Technology |
