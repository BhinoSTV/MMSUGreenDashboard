# 15.2.3 Work to maintain and extend ecosystems and their biodiversity

Max 3 pts · Year: 2024 · **Status: Strong, needs a 2024-dated record**

## Evidence statement (draft)

MMSU works directly to maintain and extend ecosystems and the biodiversity of plants
and animals on and beyond its campuses.

1. **Biodiversity Conservation Learning Center and Leisure Park, Batac main campus.**
   On 13 Jan 2023, 120 seedlings of 28 native tree species and two bamboo species were
   planted there. The area is used as a field laboratory by students and faculty
   (campus executive director Joselito Rosario). It is being developed into a health
   and wellness tourism site. `[CONFIRM: total area, species count, 2024 additions]`
2. **Salinged Park / Biodiversity Conservation Learning Center, native species.**
   Planted 27 Apr 2022: 50 native species including narra, bani, ipil, lanete,
   banaba, ylang-ylang, guijo and white lauan.
3. **Coastal ecosystems (MMSU Institute of Environmental Governance, IEG, CAFSD).**
   Oct–Nov 2021, with Tanggol Kalikasan Inc. and local governments: 10,000+ mangrove
   seedlings on 28 ha at Pasuquin; 200 on 2 ha at Currimao; ~380 bamboo propagules at
   Bangui; ~100 mango seedlings for riverbank stabilisation at Pinili.
4. **National Greening Program.** MMSU is the lead state university for a 170-hectare
   nipa plantation under the DENR National Greening Program. `[CONFIRM: year and status]`
5. **Plant genetic resources.** `[CONFIRM: Clonal Nursery inventory; indigenous food
   plant catalog launched by MMSU]`
