# 15.4.3 Policy, process or practice on hazardous waste disposal

Max 4 pts (exists 1, evidence 1, public 1, created/reviewed 2020–2024 1) ·
In place by 2024 · **Status: Partial (revised text not read)**

THE defines hazardous materials as biological, chemical, radiological or physical
agents that can harm people, animals or the environment.

## What was found

The MMSU Waste Management Program page describes an Integrated Pollution and Waste
Management Program (IPWMP) led by the PCU and General Services, referring to a
"Revised IPWMP" and related policies as downloadable documents. The page text did not
state hazardous-waste procedures, an approval date or a review date. The original
University Waste Management program was approved by the Board of Regents in 2009.
