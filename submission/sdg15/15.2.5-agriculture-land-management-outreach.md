# 15.2.5 Educational outreach on sustainable management of land for agriculture

Max 3 pts (free access 1 / charged 0.25; evidence 1; public 1) · Year: 2024 ·
**Status: Strong, confirm 2024 data and free access**

## Evidence statement (draft)

MMSU CARES (Coordinated Agribusiness, Research and Extension Strategies for Technology
Promotion and Transfer), established in 2018, transfers improved and climate-resilient
farm technologies to farmers in Ilocos Norte.

- **Demonstration farm:** 23 hectares with a solar-powered pump and irrigation canals,
  and mechanised planting and harvesting equipment donated by partner agencies.
- **Technologies promoted:** drought-tolerant or early-maturing rice (Sahod-ulan) and
  flood-tolerant rice (Tubigan); Bio-N inoculant; carrageenan plant growth promoter;
  organic fertiliser.
- **Reach:** 1,479 farmers benefited, including students taking the K-12 agriculture
  track and their parents, through training on production, processing and related
  interventions. `[CONFIRM: which year this covers, and the training dates]`
- **Access:** `[CONFIRM: training and farm inputs are free of charge to beneficiaries]`
- Related extension: organic fertiliser production through vermiculture, goat
  raising, and village-scale nipa technology. `[CONFIRM: 2024 activities]`
