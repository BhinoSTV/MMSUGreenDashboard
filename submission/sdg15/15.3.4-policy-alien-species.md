## Draft policy for adoption (not valid until approved and dated)

**Mariano Marcos State University — Alien and Invasive Species Management Policy**
Approved: `[CONFIRM]` · Resolution No.: `[CONFIRM]` · Review due: `[CONFIRM: ≤3 years]`

1. **Purpose.** Prevent the introduction and spread of alien species that harm native
   ecosystems on university land, and reduce the impact of those already present.
2. **Definition.** Alien species follow the IUCN definition: species, subspecies or
   lower taxa occurring outside their natural range and dispersal potential.
   Invasive alien species are those whose spread threatens biodiversity.
3. **Statements.**
   a. Use native species for university landscaping, restoration and greening
      (the national tree-planting lists of native species `[CONFIRM: source]`).
   b. Do not plant species listed as invasive in the Philippines
      (`[CONFIRM: reference list, e.g. DENR/BIOFIN invasive species list]`).
   c. Survey campuses each year for invasive plants and animals and record location
      and cover in a register.
   d. Remove or control listed invasive species using the least harmful method;
      dispose of plant material so it cannot re-establish.
   e. Quarantine and inspect plants and soil introduced to nurseries, research plots
      and the Clonal Nursery.
4. **Responsibilities.** CAFSD (technical), General Services (grounds), Clonal
   Nursery managers, `[CONFIRM: others]`.
5. **Monitoring and review.** Annual invasive-species register; review every three years.
6. **Approval block:** `[CONFIRM]`.

## Confirm before submitting

Ask CAFSD whether campus grounds already follow a native-species planting rule. If a
written rule exists, submit it with its date. The activity evidence (native-species
plantings) supports the policy but does not replace it.
