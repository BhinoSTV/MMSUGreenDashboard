## Draft standard for adoption (not valid until approved and dated)

**Mariano Marcos State University — Water Discharge Standards and Guidelines**
Approved: `[CONFIRM]` · Resolution/Order No.: `[CONFIRM]`

1. **Purpose.** Protect receiving waters, wildlife and human health from university
   discharges.
2. **Standard.** University discharges meet the applicable DENR effluent standards for
   the receiving water class (`[CONFIRM: current DENR administrative order and class
   of the receiving water body with counsel and PCU]`).
3. **Guidelines.**
   a. No untreated laboratory, clinical, workshop or farm effluent to drains or
      waterways.
   b. Treatment before discharge (septic or constructed wetland or
      `[CONFIRM: installed system]`), with scheduled desludging.
   c. Laboratories and workshops segregate chemical and used-oil waste from drains.
   d. Quarterly sampling of discharge points for pH, BOD, TSS and
      `[CONFIRM: parameters]`; records kept by the PCU.
   e. Non-compliance is reported to the President and corrected within
      `[CONFIRM: period]`.
4. **Responsibilities.** PCU (monitoring), General Services (operation), unit heads
   (laboratory compliance).
5. **Approval block:** `[CONFIRM]`.

## Confirm before submitting

Do not submit a standard the university does not follow. If treatment and monitoring do
not exist, treat this as an institutional gap and score zero, or adopt the standard
only with a real monitoring arrangement behind it.
