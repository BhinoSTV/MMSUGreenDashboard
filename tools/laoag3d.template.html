Laoag City — 3D Urban Model

An open-data city model of Laoag, Ilocos Norte: building footprints extruded to height, with roads, waterways and land cover. Built only from free data; works offline in any WebGL-capable browser.
⚠️
Most building heights are estimates. Free datasets carry a surveyed height or floor count for only of Laoag’s buildings; the rest are assigned heights from footprint size and building class (see Method & limitations). Use the model for form, density and spatial context, not for measurement. Switch the colour mode to Height basis to see which buildings are measured and which are assumed.

Method & limitations
  • Footprints. Overture Maps buildings theme (release {{RELEASE}}), a conflated blend of OpenStreetMap, Google Open Buildings (ML-derived from satellite imagery) and Microsoft ML Building Footprints. Footprints under 8 m² were dropped and outlines simplified to 0.3 m; courtyards (holes) are filled. Source mix matters: ML footprints can merge or split buildings.
  • Heights. Taken from the dataset when present (surveyed); otherwise floor count × 3.2 m (floors); otherwise assigned by the rules below (estimated). Estimated heights are a flat assumption per class/size, not a measurement, so they carry no building-to-building variation.
    BuildingAssumed height
    Footprint < 30 m² (sheds, kiosks)2.8 m
    House / residential, < 150 m²3.5 m
    House / residential, ≥ 150 m²6 m
    Unclassified, 30–200 / 200–600 / 600–2,000 / > 2,000 m²3.5 / 5 / 7 / 8 m
    School, commercial, retail, industrial, public6.5–7 m
    Apartments / church / hotel9.5 / 12 / 12 m
  • Land cover. ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m raster), read directly and vectorised for this page; shares are pixel counts over the whole extent, which includes sea and rivers (about % of pixels) and neighbouring municipalities. It is a single-year classification in which tree cover, orchards and crop edges can blur. Treat the percentages as an order-of-magnitude indicator, not a GreenMetric-grade open-space measurement. (Overture’s own land-cover layer was not used: its polygons overlap across generalisation levels.)
  • MMSU Campus 2 pin. The pin is the mean position of five or more Overture Places points for the College of Teacher Education, Graduate School and Laboratory Schools. It is approximate and does not show the 7.79 ha campus boundary. Campus 3 (CIT) is not pinned because the available points disagree.
  • Barangay boundaries are not drawn: the open release covers only about 51 of Laoag’s 80 barangays, so any outline would be incomplete.
  • Better data would change this model. A UAV orthomosaic with a surface model, LiDAR, or the city building inventory would replace the estimated heights; the pipeline in tools/ is built so surveyed heights simply override the estimates.
